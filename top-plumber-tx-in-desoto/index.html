What to explain and prepare

A plumber's process depends on the problem, so give a straightforward account rather than guessing at a cause. Before calling, make a short checklist:

  • Note the fixture or area involved and when the trouble started.
  • Describe what you have seen, heard, or smelled, without assuming why it is happening.
  • Mention any steps you have already taken and whether they changed anything.
  • Make the affected area accessible if it is safe and practical to do so.
  • Ask what the visit or assessment involves, and confirm any details you need before work begins.

Avoid taking pipes, fixtures, or equipment apart if you are not sure how they work. A temporary change can make it harder to describe the original problem, and an uncertain repair attempt could cause further trouble. Share what you tried instead, so the plumber has an accurate starting point.
